Founder, Builder, Operator.
Founder & CEO of Noderan, building AI agents, no-code automation systems, and practical software products for business workflows.

I am a Bilkent CTIS graduate based in Ankara, Türkiye. My work sits around AI agents, LLM applications, workflow automation, technical SEO, SaaS product development, and growth systems.

I like owning the full path from idea to launch: market thinking, product strategy, engineering execution, distribution, and the operational details that make a product work in the real world.

Current Focus
Building Noderan, an AI-powered no-code automation platform.
Designing AI agents that connect tools, execute workflows, and reduce manual operations.
Turning LLM capabilities into reliable product features with clear business value.
Growing distribution across SEO, content, product-led loops, and founder-led sales.
Operating Principles
I care about speed, but not at the cost of structural quality.
I like direct communication, clear ownership, and products that actually ship.
I enjoy building systems end-to-end: strategy, product, engineering, growth, and execution.
